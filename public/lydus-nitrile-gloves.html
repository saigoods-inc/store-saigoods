The blue 4 mil LYDUS Nitrile Examination – Standard glove is the starting point for clinical, hygiene, cleaning, food-handling, and other everyday tasks where frequent glove changes matter.

The black 5 mil LYDUS General glove adds thickness for general-purpose tasks while maintaining a practical disposable format.

Heavy Duty: the thickest LYDUS option

The black 8 mil LYDUS Heavy Duty glove is the thickest option in this collection and is intended for more demanding jobs that benefit from added material and a confident grip.
